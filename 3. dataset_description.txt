No: Row number
year, month, day, hour:	Date and time of the recorded measurement
PM2.5: Fine particulate matter ≤2.5 micrometres in diameter (µg/m³). Small enough to penetrate deep into the lungs and bloodstream.
PM10: Coarser particulate matter ≤10 micrometres in diameter (µg/m³), includes PM2.5 plus larger particles such as dust and pollen.
SO2: Sulfur dioxide concentration (µg/m³). Produced mainly by burning coal and fossil fuels.
NO2: Nitrogen dioxide concentration (µg/m³). Produced mainly by vehicle emissions and combustion.
CO: Carbon monoxide concentration (µg/m³). Produced by incomplete combustion (traffic, heating).
O3: Ground-level ozone concentration (µg/m³). Formed by chemical reactions between NO2 and sunlight, not emitted directly.
TEMP: Air temperature (degree Celsius)
PRES: Atmospheric pressure (hPa)
DEWP: Dew point temperature (°C) — temperature at which air becomes saturated with moisture
RAIN: Precipitation (mm)
wd: Wind direction, given as compass abbreviations (e.g. N = North, NE = Northeast, SW = Southwest, ENE = East-northeast). 16-point compass notation.
WSPM: Wind speed (m/s)
station: name of the air-quality monitoring site.
